Driving guide & keyboard controls

L take off / land · Q rise · E descend. WASD steers and controls speed in flight. Landing aligns you with a nearby road; L cancels landing. H returns immediately to the road.

W / ↑ accelerate · S / ↓ brake, then reverse · A D / ← → steer · Hold B to reverse
Space auto-drive · C camera · F cockpit · R biome/season · T time · V paint · G traffic
H return to road · M mute · Esc / P pause
Manual steering temporarily overrides auto-drive. Brake always takes priority. Return to Road stops the car safely in your selected lane.

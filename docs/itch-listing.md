## Page description

Take the long way. Wanderlane is a relaxed driving prototype you can play in your browser. Cruise through a stylized Pune neighbourhood route, try a short discovery drive, or switch to endless countryside roads.

### In this build

- A Pune city pilot built from offline OpenStreetMap road data.
- Baner Evening and Monsoon Pashan discovery drives, checkpoints and a session journal.
- Rickshaws, scooters, cars and buses along the curated route, with traffic signals.
- Local-style fictional shops, pedestrians and a small neighbourhood art benchmark.
- Changing time of day, seasonal looks, multiple cameras and optional auto-drive.
- Keyboard driving and on-screen touch controls.

### Controls

WASD or arrow keys: drive and steer. S / down: brake, then reverse. Hold B: reverse. Space: auto-drive. C: cycle camera. F: cockpit. G: traffic. M: mute. Esc / P: pause and settings.

For a first drive, press Begin, choose a discovery drive, and start slowly. Turn on traffic from the toolbar or with G. If performance is slow, lower quality and shadows in Settings.

### A prototype, not a surveyed city replica

This is an early playable build. Shops and decorative scenes are fictional, terrain elevation is synthetic, and traffic follows a curated route. People, vegetation, buildings and collision behaviour are still being refined. The journal lasts for the current session. A modern browser with WebGL is required; performance varies by device.

### Credits and asset disclosure

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under ODbL 1.0. Attribution and the source database offer are included in the game. One residential facade texture was generated with AI; its exact prompt and provenance ship with the build. The game uses Three.js.
